What we do

Exploring, producing, refining, and delivering the world’s essential energy resources.

Geological & Geophysical (G&G) Services:

 Utilizing cutting-edge seismic imaging and data analysis to identify and assess potential oil and natural gas reserves.

Drilling Operations:

Executing safe and efficient onshore and offshore drilling programs to access reservoirs.

Field Development & Production:

Managing the installation and operation of surface facilities and optimizing extraction processes to maximize resource recovery.

Pipeline Transportation:

Operating extensive, secure pipeline networks for the bulk movement of hydrocarbons to refineries and storage terminals.es.

Midstream Operations (Processing & Transport)

Midstream operations bridge the gap between oil and gas production and refining. This segment involves the efficient gathering, processing, storage, and transportation of hydrocarbons. We manage extensive infrastructure, including pipelines and terminals, to move crude oil and natural gas safely and reliably from production fields to markets and refineries. Our focus is on maintaining operational integrity and efficiency throughout the supply chain.

Gathering & Processing

Collecting raw natural gas and crude oil and processing them to meet market specifications.

Storage & Logistics

Managing large-scale storage facilities and coordinating global transportation via rail, truck, and tanker shipments.

Distribution & Sales:

Marketing and supplying finished petroleum products to a diverse customer base, including wholesale distributors, commercial clients, and retail fueling stations.
